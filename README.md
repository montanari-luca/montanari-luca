## 🧭 How I build things

- **Local-first.** Your data stays on your machine unless you say otherwise.
- **Bring your own everything.** Your database, your API keys, your models. No accounts with me required.
- **Native and fast.** Rust under the hood, because Electron already has enough fans. Mostly the laptop kind.
- **It has to look good.** Frosted glass, dark and light mode, smooth animations. Function first, but taste is non-negotiable.
